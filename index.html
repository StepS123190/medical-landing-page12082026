Who we are

Studio Steps creates high-quality medical and scientific animation, turning complex information into visual narratives that actually land. The work spans medical devices and pharmaceuticals through to patient education and clinical training. The focus throughout is clarity, credibility and production value: every animation needs to look compelling and stay scientifically accurate.

Our perspective

Some ideas are difficult, or impossible, to capture with live action alone.

Animation lets you show mechanisms, devices and internal processes that traditional video simply can't reach, from molecular pathways to what's happening inside the body. Audiences understand faster, retain more, and engage with more confidence.

Done well, it simplifies complex medical concepts, enhances trust through clarity, and scales across the platforms and formats your audience already expects to find you on.
